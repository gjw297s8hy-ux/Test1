# Storylines

Brief: « fais-moi une vidéo illustration qui dépote toutes tes capacités ». The 4 X reference posts could not be
opened from the build sandbox (x.com blocked by the network policy), so the grammar comes from the subject itself.

## A — Une phrase, un film (built)

Device: one object across worlds (the person's own sentence). Form: object-to-object chain.
The sentence « fais-moi une vidéo qui dépote » slams in word by word, then travels: its O opens onto a 3D world
where the word becomes 2 000+ cubes, a cube face becomes a paper sheet where the film charts its own frames,
the chart whips into a phone where the film gets posted, and the tap opens the end card.

- First 2 s: « FAIS-MOI » fills the orange frame at frame 0, then one word per beat on a new ground each time.
- Beats: hook (kinetic type) → 3D (voxels assemble) → data (count-up, bar chart of this film) → UI (post it) → end card.
- Look: screen-print poster — four flat inks, Archivo Black, misregistered second plate.
- Sound: 120 BPM pulse; silence + hits on the words, the beat drops when the 3D world opens; riser into the lockup.

## B — L'atelier en une prise

One continuous take across a desk: the camera never cuts, each capability is an object on the desk (a printed
poster, a clay cube, a ruler-chart, a phone). Calmer, 40 s, one long push.

## C — Split screen to one

Four panes play four kinds of film at once (type, 3D, data, UI), each pane cutting on the beat, then they collapse
into the one that carries the end card. Densest, most "reel", least story.
